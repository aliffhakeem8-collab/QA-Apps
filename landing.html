Core Features

Built for Compliance Teams

Audit Trail

Every test, inspection, and decision is logged. Search conversation history to prove compliance and reference past findings.

Traceability

Link findings to suppliers, batches, dates, and staff. Trace issues back instantly when problems arise.

Real-Time Alerts

Temperature excursions, failed tests, and compliance gaps trigger instant notifications for your team.

Compliance Reports

Generate HACCP, FSMA, and FDA reports directly from your audit logs. Export in seconds.

Team Access

QA managers, receiving staff, kitchen teams—everyone sees the same information, eliminating miscommunication.

Pattern Recognition

Identify trends across your testing history. Know which suppliers need closer attention before failures occur.

How Teams Use Conforma

Real Workflows, Real Impact

Receiving & Inspection

Standardize incoming checks across all deliveries. Temperature verification, visual inspection, supplier certification, and allergen protocols—all documented in one place with timestamps and traceability to batch numbers and suppliers.

QA:
Supplier delivery logged. Running standard checks.
Staff:
Temp at receiving: 39°F. Case count verified.
QA:
Temp acceptable. Visual condition?
Staff:
No damage observed. Packaging intact.
OK:
Batch approved. Moved to storage. Shelf life: 21 days.

Temperature Monitoring

Log cooler, freezer, and prep area temperatures on schedule. Automatic alerts when readings drift outside range. Complete record for health inspectors, with no manual spreadsheets.

Staff:
Walk-in cooler check. 8:00 AM reading: 37°F
OK:
Cooler #1: 37°F (Safe range)
Staff:
Freezer #2: -2°F
ALERT:
Freezer #2 above target. Technician notified.
QA:
Corrective action logged. Repair scheduled.
