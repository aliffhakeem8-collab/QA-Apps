Audit week is spent chasing records

HACCP, GMP and halal are checked separately

Different checklists and different reviewers mean gaps slip through in the handoff, and nobody sees the full picture.

Frequently asked questions

Purpose-built for food QA teams that need precision and speed.

What does Conforma check?

Labels and QA records, against HACCP, GMP, the Malaysian Food Act and halal requirements. Every item comes back as pass, review or fail, along with what Conforma found.

Who is Conforma for?

QA teams at Malaysian food manufacturers who spend hours on repetitive compliance checks and audit preparation.

Do I need to set up checklists first?

No. Conforma comes with pre-configured checklists for your industry, so you can start checking on day one.

What happens when Conforma isn't sure?

The item is marked for review instead of pass or fail. Assign it to someone on your team, resolve it, and the outcome goes into the audit log.

Can I share results with an auditor?

Every check is logged and timestamped. Generate an audit report and share it with regulators or leadership in seconds.
