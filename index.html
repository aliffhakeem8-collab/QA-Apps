( Conforma, step by step )

From upload to verdict, with a person signing off.

Conforma doesn’t replace your QA team. It does the first read, points to the regulation clause behind every finding, and leaves the decision to someone qualified to make it.

  1. i.UploadA lab report, artwork file or photo of a label. Scans are read with OCR.
  2. ii.MatchThe relevant Food Act clauses and your own reference limits are pulled in.
  3. iii.Check twiceOne model drafts the findings; a second model validates them.
  4. iv.Sign offPass, flag or fail, with reasons, ready for your QA team to approve.
