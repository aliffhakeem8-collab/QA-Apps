01  Technology

Compliance checks in minutes, not days.

Conforma reads your documents and labels, compares them with the relevant regulations and your own limits, and returns a clear verdict with the reasoning shown.

  • Lab and microbiological report checks
  • Packaging layout and artwork review
  • Photo-based label checks
Visit Conforma Request early access
How it works
  1. 1
    UploadA PDF, photo or document. Scans are read with OCR.
  2. 2
    Match regulationsRelevant Food Act clauses and your reference limits are pulled in.
  3. 3
    Draft and validateOne AI model drafts the check; a second model validates it.
  4. 4
    Clear verdictPass, flag or fail, ready for your QA team to review.
